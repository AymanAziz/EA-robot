# Data, features, models and research assumptions

Official documentation checked during implementation:

- [Coinbase Exchange candles](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles): 900-second granularity, at most 300 buckets, time/low/high/open/close/volume order. Pagination filters out candles outside the requested interval. Missing tick intervals are not filled with invented prices; integrity validation fails on in-session gaps.
- [Coinbase Exchange ticker](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-ticker): last trade timestamp and best bid/ask. Used only by the clearly labeled local simulator; an Alpaca order must use an Alpaca quote.
- [LightGBM 4.6 classifier](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/v4.6.0/pythonapi/lightgbm.LGBMClassifier.html) and [native Booster persistence](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/v4.6.0/pythonapi/lightgbm.Booster.html): CPU, one thread, deterministic and fixed seed; native text artifact, no executable pickle input.
- [scikit-learn probability calibration](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html): fit sigmoid calibration on observations disjoint from model fitting. We explicitly fit a logistic mapping of raw log-odds on a subsequent chronological calibration slice.
- [scikit-learn TimeSeriesSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html): expanding chronological folds with a five-bar purge.
- [exchange_calendars](https://github.com/gerrymanoim/exchange_calendars): XNYS regular trading sessions, holidays and early closes. Forex uses New York Sunday 17:00 through Friday 17:00 as a preliminary session filter; OANDA's instrument tradeability remains authoritative for exceptional holidays.

## Research contract

All candle timestamps are UTC **opening times**. Analysis consumes only bars whose opening time plus fifteen minutes is no later than the current time. Features use only current and previous completed bars, never backward shifts, full-sample normalization, or future quantiles. Labels measure return from the **next bar open** to the open four bars after entry. Labels crossing any session or data gap are discarded.

The usable observations are split in order: 50% training, 15% calibration, 15% threshold validation and 20% final test, with five observations purged between slices. A declared five-value probability grid is considered only on validation data. Three expanding walk-forward fits use the pre-test development interval and the **predeclared configured** probability threshold; the later-selected final threshold cannot leak into earlier folds. The final test gates deployment; it is never used to fit the booster, calibrator, or threshold. Repeatedly tuning based on rejection of the same test set would contaminate it: gather a new holdout before changing research hypotheses.

Expected gross return is the calibrated directional probability weighted by the positive/negative forward-return means in training. This estimate is intentionally simple and can be wrong. Execution still needs net edge after estimated fees, spread and slippage, permitted regime, liquidity, market hours and independent risk approval.

Each candidate records reproducibility hashes, configuration, library versions, splits, classification quality, validation/test results, benchmarks, regime results and failure reasons. Saved candidates remain inactive. Explicit promotion requires all acceptance gates: validation and test trade counts, positive validation/test returns, test return above the same-capped buy-and-hold and simple momentum baseline, drawdown within limits, positive Sharpe, Brier no worse than the training class prior, positive lower bootstrap mean trade P/L, and at least two profitable walk-forward folds. **Failure means NO TRADE.** Promotion cannot override a failed criterion. Bootstrap independence is an approximation, not proof of an edge.

Backtests execute signals at the next eligible fifteen-minute open. They call the same `decide()` and `approve_entry()` functions as the operational engine, with historical bid/ask **estimated** from the explicit spread assumption. They use finite cash, the configured initial-capital cap, risk sizing, no shorts, a 1% prior-volume participation cap outside forex, a daily-loss latch until UTC midnight, a persistent drawdown latch, estimated per-side fees/slippage, and an explicit spread. Missing expected return cannot open a trade, even at high probability. Stops, expected-return exits and the four-bar maximum holding period apply. Stop gaps fill at the worse opening price. When an OHLC bar touches both stop and target, stop executes first. A stop is active on the entry bar. `run_backtest()` itself validates and removes incomplete/closed-session candles.

New entries are also denied if the known calendar would close before the four-bar holding horizon can expire. This excludes planned overnight/weekend holdings without consulting future prices. Unexpected halts or exceptional provider holidays still require broker tradeability and may delay exits.

Validation is performed for each instrument in isolation. The operational risk engine additionally considers simultaneous positions and reservations across all brokers and correlation groups, so it may reject opportunities that the isolated test would permit. Historical bid/ask estimates, an idealized next-open execution clock, and OHLC monitoring cannot reproduce actual paper broker fills or the simulator's minute-by-minute observations. Slippage is an assumption accounting for some delay, not a guarantee of equivalent fills.

Historical bid/ask, order-book queues, dynamic crypto fee tiers, forex financing and corporate action effects are not fully represented by free OHLCV data. Forex tick count is not executable notional liquidity. CAGR is omitted for histories shorter than a year. The fixed present-day six-instrument universe does not include a delisted-asset study; these results cannot establish broad market or survivorship-unbiased performance. Provider availability and regional restrictions may prevent collection. No data is synthesized to bypass an outage.

## Artifact trust

The model folder is trusted application-owned storage. Only locally trained native LightGBM text and JSON are loaded. SHA-256 manifests detect accidental corruption, and the active pointer pins the entire metadata file. The validation-policy fingerprint pins effective risk/execution settings, all instrument assumptions, and the source of the decision, sizing, data, feature, model, calendar and backtest modules. Changing policy requires retraining; replacing credentials or dashboard settings does not. Candidates are content-versioned and committed atomically. Repeating an identical training request returns the original candidate without overwriting its artifacts or refreshing its original training timestamp. They are not signatures protecting against a malicious administrator who can rewrite the complete manifest. Restrict filesystem access and back up model folders together with the database. Model age and training-data age are checked again on inference.

## Online forecast diagnostics

`monitor_predictions(db, symbol, candles, now)` evaluates real stored predictions against matured, completed-candle labels from the same next-open/four-bar horizon as training. It excludes all candidate training, calibration, validation and acceptance-test history; predictions recorded before training or after the outcome; unknown model metadata; missing outcomes; and invalid data. Scores are grouped by model version over the available candle window, capped at500 recent observations each. Health records `model_performance:SYMBOL` expose observation count, Brier score, directional accuracy, mean **gross forward return** and the reference score. Forward returns describe observed prices, not executed strategy P/L or profitability.

At least30 matured observations are required before a model can receive a degraded diagnostic. The observed Brier score must exceed the saved out-of-sample Brier reference (or recorded class-prior Brier, or the declared neutral .5-probability benchmark) by .05, and its approximate lower95% bound must also exceed the reference. Overlapping labels are dependent, so this bound is a diagnostic approximation. These records do not activate/deactivate models or bypass validation, age checks, controls or risk limits. Initial operation correctly reports warming-up until enough actual post-deployment outcomes exist.
