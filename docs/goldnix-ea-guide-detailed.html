Build the context.

The previous 20 completed candles define the channel. The newest candle is excluded from those boundaries.

CURRENT ACTIONWAIT FOR CLOSE
01 — Signal

Close outside the range.

BUY when the latest midpoint close is strictly above the prior 20 highs. SELL when it is strictly below the prior 20 lows. Equal to the boundary means no breakout.

02 — Volatility

Size the stop with ATR.

Simple ATR(14) measures recent true ranges, including gaps from the previous close. Normal stop distance is max(2 × ATR, 0.50). The nominal target distance is 3 × that stop distance.

03 — Execution

Trade only a fresh signal.

The EA looks for a fresh completed minute, with a 10-second entry window and a recent quote. It does not buy every minute or replay signals missed during sleep or a disconnection.

About fakeouts: waiting for a close filters wick-only breaks. A close beyond the channel can still reverse and lose. The current M1 EA has no higher-timeframe trend filter, volume confirmation, RSI/MACD filter, or retest confirmation. Those were separate research ideas, not features silently added to this build. The animated scenarios assume the other account, session and permission checks pass.

There are more checks before an order: exact demo-account identity, a connected feed, Algo Trading permissions, no other position or order, a valid market session, daily budget, a fresh quote, affordable margin and legal stop distance. MT5 calculates the stop risk again using the actual entry side. Confirmed positions are checked for volume and server SL/TP. For BUY, the stop and target are anchored to Bid; for SELL, to Ask. The entry pays the spread, so a nominal 3R target is not a guaranteed net 3:1 reward/risk ratio. Gaps, fees and fills can exceed planned loss.

06 / What keeps the EA under control

The rules keep running.

M1 sets the candle interval. It does not create a one-minute holding period or a fixed daily start and stop time.

1

Position at a time

No new entry while another position or order exists. An ownership lock prevents a duplicate EA instance using the same data folder.

7

Attempts per Malaysia day

An attempt is consumed before sending an order. Reopening MT5 or switching between the M5/M1 variants does not refill that budget.

−2R

Realized entry halt

New entries stop at −2R for the risk day. A separate 10 USC equity-loss guard also blocks entries and attempts to close owned exposure.

100

Bars after start or reconnect

Capture resumes, history warms, and the EA waits for a fresh candle. Existing owned exposure is reconciled and closed when trading is allowed.

What happens if MT5 closes or the laptop sleeps?

Local capture and management pause. Existing broker-side SL/TP remain, but shutdown is not a guaranteed market exit. On restart, the EA checks its saved state, reconciles owned exposure, warms history and skips missed signals. A corrupt checkpoint or ambiguous order blocks new entries for review. Keep MT5 open, connected and the Mac awake with its lid open for continuous observation.

Are there really no operating hours?

There is no user-defined daily start time or 06:30 cutoff. Broker market sessions, a two-minute close buffer, Friday controls and holiday exclusions still restrict orders. The EA stops Friday entries from 19:00 UTC and attempts to flatten owned exposure from 20:00 UTC. Capture can continue while it waits. A new Malaysia risk day resets budgets only after previous owned exposure and pending intent are reconciled and flat.

What is logged for our later review?

Raw captured quotes, completed M1 bid/ask candles, every signal and skip reason, confirmed entry receipts, reconciled exits, and connection or restart events. Entry and exit records share a magic ID. The review reports the one-time execution check separately from normal strategy entries and separates realized P/L from an open position's floating P/L.
